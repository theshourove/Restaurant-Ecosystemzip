DISCOUNT STACKING ORDER (CRITICAL)
Subtotal = sum of (price × qty)
Member Discount = Subtotal × (tier_percent / 100) — applied automatically when member is linked
After Member = Subtotal - Member Discount
Coupon Discount = coupon_type=="percent" ? (AfterMember × coupon_value/100) : coupon_value
After Coupon = AfterMember - Coupon Discount
Manual Discount = manual_type=="percent" ? (AfterCoupon × manual_value/100) : manual_value
Capped at maxCashierDiscountPercent or maxCashierDiscountAmount from settings
After All Discounts = AfterCoupon - Manual Discount (minimum 0, cannot go negative)
Tax = taxEnabled ? (AfterAllDiscounts × taxRate/100) : 0
Delivery Fee = (orderType=="Delivery" && deliveryFeeEnabled) ? deliveryFee : 0
TOTAL = AfterAllDiscounts + Tax + DeliveryFee
